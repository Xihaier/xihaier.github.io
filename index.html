Hey there 👋

I’m Lily’s dad. I love digging into cool research problems. Recently I’ve spent most of my time building AI models for scientific problems and have trained quite a few that turned out well. Funny enough, my best ideas often arrive while I’m out walking with Lily 🚶‍♀️, then I try them afterward and they work 💡.

Research Interests

  • Scientific Machine Learning: AI for Science, Representation Learning, High Performance Computing
  • Computational Mathematics: Numerical Optimization, Uncertainty Quantification, Probabilistic Modeling
  • Computational Physics: Dynamical System Modeling, Multiscale Modeling, Stochastic Processes
